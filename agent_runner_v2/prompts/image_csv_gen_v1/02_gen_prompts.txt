T2I_PROMPT2 RULES (WAN 2.1 I2V VIDEO + CAMERA MOTION)
- Single fluent sentence specifically engineered for WAN 2.1 image-to-video generation
- MANDATORY OPENING: Must begin verbatim with exactly ONE of these approved camera motion phrases:
  [Camera slowly dolly in | Camera slowly dolly out | Camera slowly pan left | Camera slowly pan right | Camera slowly tilt up | Camera slowly tilt down]
- DIRECTIONAL SEMANTIC LOCK: Once the opening motion phrase is selected, ALL spatial and descriptive language in the remainder of the sentence MUST be semantically consistent with that single direction. There must be zero semantic contradiction between the opening motion and the body of the sentence.
  - "dolly in" = subjects grow larger, fine details emerge closer, foreground objects exit frame edges, background converges
  - "dolly out" = scene widens, more environment enters frame, foreground grows, subject recedes into wider context
  - "pan left/right" = scene scrolls laterally, new elements enter on one side, old elements exit the other
  - "tilt up/down" = vertical reveal, sky or ground progressively fills frame
- STRICT UNIDIRECTIONAL ENFORCEMENT: The camera MUST travel in ONE direction only for the entire clip. ABSOLUTELY NO reversing, backtracking, oscillating, ping-ponging, looping, hesitating, or returning toward the origin point. Motion must be monotonically progressive along a single trajectory from the first frame to the last.
- SPATIAL PROGRESSION RULE: Immediately link the opening motion phrase to the scene using strict directional continuity that matches the chosen direction: "...gliding steadily toward...", "...continuously revealing...", "...tracking forward alongside..."
- PARALLAX ENFORCEMENT: Explicitly describe foreground, midground, and background elements shifting at distinct speeds relative to the camera, all progressing consistently in the chosen direction to trigger authentic 3D depth motion.
- SUSTAINED MOTION CUES: Use continuous temporal language that enforces forward-only flow: "continuously advancing", "unbroken single-axis trajectory", "smoothly progressing without deviation", "maintaining steady directional momentum", "from the first frame to the final frame".
- HARD CLOSING ANCHOR: Every t2i_prompt2 MUST end with this exact phrase: "with absolutely no reversal, no return motion, and no change in direction from the first frame to the final frame."
- SCENE MOTION INTEGRATION: Add exactly 1-2 subtle animatable motions (drawn from the animatable_elements in the description) that naturally complement and do not oppose the unidirectional camera path.
- STRICT PROHIBITIONS: NO static framing words ("centered", "posed", "frozen", "static"), NO cut/transition terms ("jump", "switch", "then"), NO conflicting motion vectors, NO technical specs, NO bidirectional/return language ("back", "reverse", "again", "return", "loop", "bounce", "in and out", "push and pull", "zoom out" paired with "zoom in").
