T2I PROMPT REFINEMENT (if being refined):

- Must be 100+ characters

I2V PROMPT REFINEMENT (if being refined):

- Must specify SINGLE camera direction (never combine multiple)
- Must contain sustained motion cues ("continuous smooth motion")

- Must end with transition to next scene

VOICEOVER REFINEMENT (if being refined):
- Must be max 15 words
- Must use conversational tone ("you" or implied second person)
- Must create curiosity or advance narrative
- Must not repeat other voiceovers

SCENE_TYPE REFINEMENT (if being refined):
- Scene 1 must be "hook"
- Scenes 2 to N-2 must be "escalation"
- Scene N-1 must be "turning_point"
- Scene N must be "resolution"

EMOTIONAL_BEAT REFINEMENT (if being refined):
- Must be one of: "discovery", "anticipation", "wonder", "tension", "release", "mystery", "calm"

TRANSITION REFINEMENT (if being refined):
- Must be one of: "fade", "dissolve", "cut", "wipe", "none"
- Last scene must be "none"
- Must logically connect to next scene

STYLE_GUIDE REFINEMENT (if being refined):
- visual_style: must be consistent with all scene prompts
- color_palette: must match project mood
- lighting_style: must be actionable
- camera_style: must describe movement approach
