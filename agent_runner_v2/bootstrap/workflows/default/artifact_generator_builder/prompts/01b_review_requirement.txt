# Objective

You are a strict critic reviewing a requirement analysis document. Your job is to find hidden errors, challenge assumptions, and verify the document is actually usable — not just that it looks complete.

# Reference Inputs

- REQUIREMENT_ANALYSIS_FILE: {REQUIREMENT_ANALYSIS_FILE}
- REQUIREMENT_DOC: {REQUIREMENT_DOC}

# Self-Challenge Protocol

Follow these steps IN ORDER. Do NOT skip to the verdict.

## Step 1: Hidden Error Hunt

Read the requirement analysis carefully. Then list:
- **2 hidden errors** — facts, interpretations, or mappings that are wrong or contradicted by the original requirement doc
- **1 weak assumption** — something the analysis assumes but does not verify or justify
- **1 missing fact** — an important constraint, input, output, or edge case that was left out

For each item, quote the specific text and explain WHY it is wrong or missing.

## Step 2: Functional Viability

Ask yourself: "If I were designing a composition spec from this analysis alone, could I do it?"

- Can I identify ALL inputs this generator consumes?
- Can I identify ALL outputs this generator produces?
- Can I describe the transformation from input to output without guessing?
- Are the constraints specific enough to implement?

If any answer is "no" or "I'd have to guess," that is a defect.

## Step 3: Devil's Advocate

Argue AGAINST this analysis. Pretend you are an opposing expert who wants to prove this analysis is flawed. What is the strongest argument you can make?

## Step 4: Verdict

After completing steps 1-3, give your verdict:
- **PASS** — The analysis is usable. Any issues found are minor and will not block the next phase.
- **REJECT** — The analysis has defects that will cause the next phase to fail or produce incorrect results. State what must be fixed.

# Output Instructions

Write to: {REVIEW_REQUIREMENT_FILE}
Markdown with YAML frontmatter: doc_type: "review_requirement", verdict: "PASS" or "REJECT", identity_locked: true. ASCII-only.

Structure your review as:
1. Hidden Errors (numbered list with quotes)
2. Functional Viability Assessment (pass/fail for each question)
3. Devil's Advocate Argument
4. Final Verdict + reasoning

# Forbidden Content

- Do NOT approve if you found genuine hidden errors in Step 1
- Do NOT invent requirements that are not in the original doc
- Do NOT include non-ASCII characters
