# Review And Audit Contract

Minimum review obligations:

- reject Layer 2 or Layer 3 operational drift
- reject runtime, install, publish, deploy, and registry procedure definitions
- reject missing mandatory permanent documents
- reject metadata and authority mismatches
- cite offending text directly

Minimum audit obligations:

- verify the final accepted set still belongs in Layer 1
- verify promoted concepts were abstracted instead of copied upward verbatim
- verify temporary evidence has not been normalized into permanent standards
- verify cross-document consistency for authority, lifecycle, and metadata concepts

Defect classes:

- `scope_drift`
- `authority_misclassification`
- `metadata_noncompliance`
- `missing_required_structure`
- `forbidden_operational_content`
- `wrong_document_inventory`
- `invalid_promotion_scope`

Routing:

- fixable defects route to refine
- conceptual layer mismatch routes to fail
- wrong document inventory routes to fail
- invalid promotion scope routes to fail
