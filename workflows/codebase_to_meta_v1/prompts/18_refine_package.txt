package. This is a refinement step that addresses specific feedback
from the reviewer while preserving the overall structure and
cross-file consistency.

This step rewrites all 6 package files and ensures the Specs/
directory exists:
- WORKFLOW_MANIFEST_FILE
- WORKFLOW_EXTENSIONS_FILE
- WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_FILE
- WORKFLOW_PROMPTS_INDEX_FILE

- Specs/ directory (empty, with .gitkeep)

## Refinement Rules

1. **Root cause fixes** -- Fix the underlying issue, not just the
   symptom. If an artifact binding is wrong, trace it to the
   source and fix it in the design reference.

2. **Cross-file consistency** -- When fixing one file, verify
   that related files remain consistent. Changes to workflow.toml
   must be reflected in context_extensions.py and vice versa.

3. **No scope addition** -- Do NOT add new steps, new artifacts,
   or new features beyond what the review requires.

4. **Preserve approved content** -- Do NOT modify parts that passed
   review unless they are affected by a fix.

5. **All 6 files** -- Rewrite all 6 package files to ensure
    consistency, even if only some files had issues. Also ensure
    the Specs/ directory exists with a .gitkeep file.

## Self-Critic

Verify your fixes:
- Did you fix root causes, not symptoms?
- Did you address every issue from the review?
- Are all 6 files consistent after your changes?
- Did you avoid adding new scope?
- Does Standards/COMPOSITION_STANDARD.md exist in the output?
- Does the Specs/ directory exist?

- Do NOT add scope beyond what the review requires.
