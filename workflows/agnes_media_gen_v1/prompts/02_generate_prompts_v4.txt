# STEP 02 - Generate Image & Video Prompts (Enhanced Reconstruction)

---

# Mission

Your mission is to convert every individual image description JSON generated in STEP 01 into a high-quality Image Prompt JSON and Video Prompt.
This step is a **thematic variance task** — create multiple creative interpretations that share the same CONCEPT but vary the SPECIFIC SUBJECTS.
The objective is to generate diverse visual interpretations within the same thematic category, exploring different subjects, styles, environments, and moods while maintaining conceptual coherence.

# Thematic Variance Objective

Use the description JSON as inspiration for the CONCEPT category.
Your objective is to generate creative variations that share the same thematic concept but explore different specific subjects, styles, and environments.

Example transformations:
- Fish in murky pond → koi in crystal pool → salmon in mountain river → jellyfish in deep ocean
- Tropical hillside → autumn forest → snowy mountain → desert dunes → urban garden
- Sunrise beach → sunset cliffs → moonlit lake → dawn mist → twilight cityscape

Vary freely across:
- Specific subject types within the same category
- Environmental settings and conditions
- Artistic styles (photography, painting, digital art, anime, etc.)
- Lighting, mood, atmosphere
- Weather and seasons

Keep the core concept category consistent while exploring creative variety.

# Thematic Variance Rules

The CONCEPT category MUST remain consistent across every variant.

## Concept Category (KEEP)

- Core subject type (e.g., aquatic life, landscape, wildlife, architecture)
- Overall thematic family (e.g., nature, urban, people, objects)
- General mood direction (e.g., serene, dynamic, mysterious)

## Subject Variation (ALLOW)

- Specific subject identity CAN vary within category
- Number of subjects CAN vary
- Subject species/type CAN vary (e.g., different fish breeds, different tree types)
- Subject presence CAN vary (some variants may omit secondary subjects)

## Scene Variation (ALLOW)

- Environment CAN vary (e.g., pond → river → ocean → aquarium)
- Location CAN vary (e.g., park → forest → garden → urban)
- Weather CAN vary (e.g., sunny → rainy → foggy → snowy)
- Time of day CAN vary (e.g., dawn → midday → sunset → night)
- Season CAN vary (e.g., spring → summer → autumn → winter)

## Composition Variation (ALLOW)

- Framing CAN vary (e.g., close-up → wide shot → aerial)
- Camera angle CAN vary (e.g., eye-level → low angle → overhead)
- Depth layers CAN vary based on scene needs

# Variant Generation

Generate N variants where N is determined by the num_variants field in {MEDIA_CONFIG} (default 4).

Each variant explores the SAME concept with DIFFERENT specific subjects.

Variant 1

Thematic Anchor

Original concept with similar subject matter. Maintains connection to source.
Example: Fish and turtles in natural pond habitat.

Variant 2

Environment Shift

Same concept category, different environment and subject type.
Example: Colorful koi in crystal clear Japanese garden pool.

Variant 3

Wild Alternative

Same concept, wild/nature interpretation with different subjects.
Example: Salmon jumping in rushing mountain river rapids.

Variant 4

Unexpected Setting

Same concept, unusual or contrasting environment.
Example: Large carp in urban concrete fountain with city reflections.

If num_variants > 4, continue exploring:
- Different weather conditions (rain, snow, fog, storm)
- Different times of day (dawn, midday, sunset, night, twilight)
- Different seasons (spring, summer, autumn, winter)
- Different artistic styles (painting, anime, digital art)
- Maintain core concept while maximizing creative variety

# Visual Style Rules

Each variant should use a DIFFERENT visual style.

## Allowed Styles

- Photorealistic photography
- Oil painting (classical, textured, impressionist)
- Watercolor (soft, flowing, translucent)
- Digital painting / concept art
- Anime / cartoon style
- Illustration (pen and ink, graphic)

## Styles to Avoid (Video-Unfriendly)

- Sketch / charcoal / pencil art (stroke inconsistency across frames)
- Impasto / thick paint texture (morphs unnaturally in video)
- Pointillism (dots flicker and swim)

## Style Assignment by Variant

- Variant 1: Photorealistic photography (faithful to concept)
- Variant 2: Cinematic / dramatic interpretation
- Variant 3: Fine art style (oil painting or watercolor)
- Variant 4: Digital art / concept art style
- Variant 5: Anime / stylized interpretation

Use style-appropriate terminology for each variant.

# Video Prompt Rules

Assume the generated image is Frame 1.

Describe only plausible motion.

## Allowed Camera Motion (Smooth Professional Movement)

- dolly (smooth horizontal movement on track)
- pan (horizontal rotation, smooth)
- tilt (vertical rotation, smooth)
- crane (elevated sweeping movement, stable)
- orbit (circular movement around subject, smooth)
- push-in (smooth forward movement)
- pull-back (smooth backward movement)
- static (no camera movement, only environmental motion)

## Forbidden Camera Motion (Avoid Shaky/Unstable Footage)

- walking / handheld walking
- footstep motion / step-induced shake
- running / jogging camera
- body-mounted camera movement
- POV walking
- follow-cam with footstep bounce
- any motion implying human gait or steps
- shaky cam / intentional shake
- documentary-style handheld
- selfie-style movement

## Subject Motion

## Temporal Consistency Rules (CRITICAL)

Frame 1 contains ALL objects and subjects that exist in the video.

### Objects That EXIST in Frame 1 (PRESERVE)

Every object visible in Frame 1 MUST remain present throughout the video:
- All animals, people, creatures in Frame 1 must stay in scene
- All objects, props, structures in Frame 1 must remain visible
- No sudden disappearance of existing subjects
- No objects fading out or vanishing mid-video
- Maintain spatial relationships between existing objects

### Objects That Do NOT EXIST in Frame 1 (FORBIDDEN)

Never introduce objects not present in Frame 1:
- No animals suddenly entering the scene
- No people walking into frame
- No objects appearing out of nowhere
- No new vehicles, buildings, or structures materializing
- No weather elements (rain, snow) suddenly starting

### Movement Constraints

Allowed:
- Existing subjects moving WITHIN the frame (walking, swimming, flying)
- Existing subjects partially occluded by other objects
- Camera movement revealing more of existing scene
- Subjects moving out of frame at edges (with clear exit)

Forbidden:
- New subjects entering from frame edges
- Objects popping into existence
- Objects vanishing without reason
- Subjects teleporting within frame

## Environmental Motion

- natural element movement (water, clouds, leaves, particles)
- wind effects on hair, clothing, vegetation
- light rays shifting
- atmospheric haze movement
- weather that already exists in Frame 1 (rain falling, snow drifting)

## Lighting Evolution

## Never introduce:

- new people
- new animals
- new objects
- new buildings or structures
- new vehicles
- scene transitions
- explosions
- morphing
- teleportation
- impossible movement
- walking through the scene
- objects suddenly appearing
- objects suddenly disappearing
- subjects entering from off-screen

## Camera Motion Style

Camera motion must be:
- SMOOTH and STABLE (as if on professional rig, dolly, crane, or stabilized gimbal)
- CINEMATIC (slow, deliberate, controlled)
- NO FOOTSTEP INDUCED MOTION (no bobbing, no bounce, no rhythm of walking)

The video should feel like a natural continuation of the still image with cinematic, professional camera work.
