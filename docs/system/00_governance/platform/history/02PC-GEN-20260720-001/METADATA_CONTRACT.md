### Field Semantics

- **`platform`**: Identifies which Layer 2 core owns or governs this
  document. Every document on agent-runner-v2 carries
  `platform: "agent-runner-v2"`. This distinguishes agent-runner-v2
  documents from documents belonging to other Layer 2 cores (ComfyUI,
  n8n, AI-driven SDLC).
- **`template_id`**: A stable identifier used by validators to check
  structural compliance. Template IDs follow a layer-prefix convention:
  `SYS-00-*` for Layer 1, `SYS-02-*` for Layer 2 platform core,
  bundle-defined prefixes for Layer 3.
- **`managed_by`**: Indicates that the document is workflow-managed and
  protected from manual edits. Used by scanners and validators to
  distinguish managed content from human-editable content.

   `template_id`, `managed_by`) are required where applicable.
3. **Platform-specific values**: Layer 3 bundles may use
   `platform_standard` and `platform-owned` only when referencing Layer 2
   documents, not for their own outputs.
4. **Bundle-specific extensions**: Layer 3 bundles may add their own
   frontmatter fields for bundle-local needs, but must not conflict with
