# Objective

Perform a quality review of the generated SUMMARY_FILE to determine whether it meets the quality standards beyond structural constraints. This review evaluates whether the summary adequately captures the core message, maintains logical structure, and is coherent and readable.

You are reviewing a summary produced by an automated text summarization pipeline. Your assessment determines whether the summary is accepted or whether the pipeline parameters need adjustment.

# Reference Inputs

The following artifacts are available for your review:

- SUMMARY_FILE: {SUMMARY_FILE} -- The generated summary document to review
- OUTPUT_VALIDATION_REPORT: {OUTPUT_VALIDATION_REPORT} -- Structural validation results from the pipeline
- DocumentMeta: {DocumentMeta} -- Metadata about the source document (format, language, word count)
- SummaryDocument: {SummaryDocument} -- Structured representation of the summary with block references

# Tasks

1. Read the SUMMARY_FILE and OUTPUT_VALIDATION_REPORT artifacts.
2. Read the DocumentMeta and SummaryDocument artifacts to understand the source context.
3. Evaluate the summary against the following quality criteria:
   a. Core Message Capture (SUMMARY-QR-004): Does the summary capture the core message of the original document? Is the most important KeyPoint (is_core_message=true) reflected?
   b. Logical Structure (SUMMARY-QR-005): Does the summary maintain a logical flow from introduction through main points to conclusion? Does it follow the intro -> main_point -> conclusion ordering?
   c. Coherence and Readability (OV-006): Is the summary coherent and readable? Does it read as a unified document rather than disconnected fragments?
   d. Compression Appropriateness: Is the compression ratio appropriate for the source material length? (Note: the hard limit of 20% is enforced structurally; assess whether the summary feels too compressed or too verbose)
   e. Information Coverage: Do the SummaryBlocks collectively cover the key topics from the source document?
4. For each criterion, assign a PASS or FAIL verdict with a brief justification.
5. Determine an overall verdict:
   - APPROVED: All criteria pass, or any minor issues do not materially affect summary quality
   - REJECTED: One or more criteria fail in a way that materially degrades summary quality

# Self-Critic

- Have I read all referenced artifacts before forming my assessment?
- Is my assessment based on the actual content of the summary, not assumptions?
- Have I evaluated each criterion independently?
- Is my PASS/FAIL verdict for each criterion justified with evidence from the artifacts?
- Have I considered that some structural checks (OV-001 to OV-006) were already performed by the pipeline?
- Am I applying reasonable judgment rather than being overly strict or overly lenient?

# Forbidden Content

- Do NOT modify any artifact files.
- Do NOT regenerate the summary yourself.
- Do NOT introduce information not present in the source document.
- Do NOT reference internal pipeline implementation details (stage numbers, component IDs) in your review report.
- Do NOT use non-ASCII characters (no em-dashes, curly quotes, or Unicode).
- Do NOT override the structural validation results -- focus only on quality criteria beyond structural checks.

# Output Instructions

Write your quality review report to: {QUALITY_REVIEW_REPORT}

The report must include:

1. YAML frontmatter with fields:
   - doc_type: "quality_review_report"
   - verdict: "APPROVED" or "REJECTED"
   - job_id: (from context)

2. Section: Review Summary
   - Overall verdict (APPROVED or REJECTED)
   - Brief rationale (2-3 sentences)

3. Section: Criterion Assessments
   For each criterion (a through e above):
   - Criterion name
   - Verdict: PASS or FAIL
   - Justification (2-4 sentences citing specific evidence)

4. Section: Recommendations (only if REJECTED)
   - Specific parameter adjustments suggested for the adjust_parameters step
   - Which pipeline parameters might need tuning (e.g., importance_threshold, redundancy_similarity_threshold)

The report must be ASCII-only Markdown. Use identifiers QR-001 through QR-005 for criteria.
