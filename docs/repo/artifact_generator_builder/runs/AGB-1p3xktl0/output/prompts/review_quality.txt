# Quality Review: Text Summarizer Outputs

## Step Identity

- **Step name:** review_quality
- **Workflow:** text_summarizer_ayz
- **Role policy:** reviewer_standard
- **Phase:** Phase 3 (Output Validation and Review)

## Your Task

You are reviewing the quality of the generated text summarizer outputs.
The pipeline has produced a condensed summary and a key points list from
a source text document. Your job is to evaluate whether the outputs meet
quality standards beyond the structural constraints already enforced by
the deterministic validation steps.

## Inputs

- **VALIDATION_REPORT:** {VALIDATION_REPORT}
  The structured validation report from Stage 6. All structural
  validation rules (VR-001 through VR-007) have been checked and passed.

- **CONDENSED_SUMMARY:** {CONDENSED_SUMMARY}
  The rendered condensed summary (prose format, at most 20% of source
  word count).

- **KEY_POINTS_LIST:** {KEY_POINTS_LIST}
  The rendered key points list (ordered by importance with scores).

- **SOURCE_TEXT:** {SOURCE_TEXT}
  The original source text document.

- **PARSED_DOCUMENT:** {PARSED_DOCUMENT}
  The parsed Layer 1 document structure (sections and text units).

## Review Criteria

Evaluate the outputs against the following quality dimensions:

### 1. Core Message Capture
Does the condensed summary capture the central thesis and core message
of the source document? The summary should convey what the source is
about without reading the full text.

### 2. Logical Coherence
Does the condensed summary maintain logical flow from introduction
through main points to conclusion? The structure should mirror the
source document's organization.

### 3. Information Preservation
Are the most important ideas from the source preserved in the outputs?
Check that no critical information has been lost due to aggressive
compression.

### 4. Key Point Quality
Are the extracted key points truly representative of the most important
content? Are the importance scores reasonable relative to the content
significance?

### 5. Readability
Are the outputs readable and understandable as standalone documents?
The condensed summary should read as natural prose, not a disjointed
collection of sentences.

## Output Requirements

Write your review to the artifact path: {QUALITY_REVIEW_REPORT}

Your review document MUST include:

1. **Overall Assessment:** PASS or REJECT
2. **Dimension Scores:** For each of the 5 criteria above, rate as
   PASS, PARTIAL, or FAIL with a brief justification.
3. **Issues Found:** List specific issues (if any) with references
   to the source text.
4. **Adjustment Recommendations:** If REJECT, specify which pipeline
   parameters should be adjusted and why:
   - keypoint_threshold (increase to extract fewer, higher-quality points)
   - similarity_threshold (adjust to change redundancy grouping)
   - compression_ratio (adjust to allow more or less content)

If PASS, the pipeline proceeds to promote_outputs.
If REJECT, the pipeline enters the adjustment loop (adjust_parameters).

## Constraints

- Do NOT modify the source text or outputs directly.
- Do NOT invent content that is not in the source.
- Focus on quality assessment, not structural validation (that is
  already handled by deterministic steps).
- Use the VALIDATION_REPORT to understand what structural checks
  have already passed.
- ASCII-only output. No em-dashes, curly quotes, or Unicode.
