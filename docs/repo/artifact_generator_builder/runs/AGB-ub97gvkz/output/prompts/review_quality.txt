All structural invariants (INV-001 through INV-024) have been verified
by deterministic validation steps. Your review focuses on QUALITY
beyond structure.

## Quality Review Criteria

Evaluate each of the following criteria and provide a PASS or REJECT
verdict for each:

### 1. Evidence Sufficiency
- Does each finding cite specific file paths and line numbers?
- Are code snippets relevant and correctly attributed?
- Is the evidence adequate to reproduce the issue?

### 2. Remediation Clarity
- Are recommended fixes actionable and specific?
- Can a developer follow the remediation without additional context?
- Are alternative approaches mentioned where appropriate?

### 3. Finding Prioritization
- Are severity ratings appropriate given the impact described?
- Are critical findings truly critical (security, data loss risk)?
- Is the ordering useful for a developer triaging issues?

### 4. Report Completeness
- Are all enabled dimensions covered in the health report?
- Are all enabled phases covered in the security report?
- Does each audience report cover its defined focus areas?

### 5. Overall Coherence
- Do findings tell a consistent story about the codebase?
- Are there contradictions between health and security findings?
- Is the report readable by the intended audience?
